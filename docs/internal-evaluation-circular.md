# Internal FYP Evaluation — Circular

**Institution:** Sharif College of Engineering and Technology (SCET)
**Department:** Computer Science — Session 2022
**Circular date:** 07 June 2026

Internal Final Year Project (FYP) Evaluation for all Final Year Computer Science
students.

## Schedule

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 15 July 2026 |
| **Time** | 10:00 am – 01:00 pm |
| **Venue** | Auditorium |

## Instructions (Strictly Followed)

1. **Professional Dress Code** — Formal attire in subtle, professional colors.
   Bright/flashy colors, casual clothing, jeans, T-shirts, slippers, or other
   inappropriate outfits are not permitted.
2. **Reporting Time** — Arrive at the venue at least **30 minutes** before your
   scheduled presentation time.
3. **Presentation Readiness** — Ensure your PPT is complete, well-rehearsed, and
   compatible with the presentation system. Keep a **backup on USB and cloud
   storage**.
4. **Project Demonstration** — Be prepared to demonstrate your project and answer
   technical questions from the evaluation panel.
5. **Required Materials** — Bring your laptop, charger, VGA/HDMI cable, Wi-Fi dongle
   (if required), and any additional hardware/accessories needed for the demo.
6. **Professional Conduct** — Maintain discipline, remain respectful, and avoid
   unnecessary movement or conversations during the sessions.
7. **Attendance** — Mandatory. Absence without prior departmental approval will be
   dealt with per departmental regulations.
8. **Mobile Phones** — Keep all phones on silent mode throughout the session.
9. **Time Slot Compliance** — Be available in the assigned time slot provided by the
   FYP Coordinator.

> Students are advised to take this evaluation seriously and present themselves in a
> professional manner, as it reflects both their technical competence and
> professional ethics.

*Issued by: Head of Department, Department of Computer Science, Sharif College of
